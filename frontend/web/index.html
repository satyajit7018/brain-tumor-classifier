🛡️ Clinical Model Card & Regulatory Safety Disclaimers

1. Operational Scope & Safety Intent
Designed as an algorithmic explainability and deep learning benchmarking platform. Strictly prohibited for automated standalone diagnostic triage without certified radiologist review.


2. Clinical False Negative Prioritization
In clinical neuro-oncology, a False Negative (predicting no tumor when pathology exists) is penalized via inverse-frequency class weighting to minimize missed diagnoses.


3. Bayesian Uncertainty Safety Thresholds
Selective Monte Carlo Dropout ($N=20$) is restricted to the dense head to preserve batch normalization statistics. Predictions triggering $H \ge 0.50$ or $\sigma_{\max} \ge 0.35$ automatically escalate to HIGH_RISK_RADIOLOGIST_REVIEW.
